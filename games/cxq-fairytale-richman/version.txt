CxQ Fairy-tale Richman — testing branch 20260825-2010

Release status
- This build belongs only to preview/cxq-fairytale-richman-testing.
- The main branch remains unchanged until the user approves the testing build.
- The only runtime path is index.html -> core.js + viewport.js + game.js. The obsolete home-richman4 overlay and temporary patch files are removed.

Art and presentation
- Dice throws now use six independently generated physical ivory-and-gold dice without the former floating cushion. The throw travels from the action console into the board, rotates in flight, bounces, settles and only then presents the result.
- All three board worlds now use independently generated loop-road backgrounds authored for gameplay readability; movement nodes share the visible road centerline and the map selector/minimap use the same world art.
- All ten CxQ characters now have an independently generated completed-region landmark. A completed region promotes one anchor property to its owner's character landmark while the remaining properties retain their map building progression.
- Property ownership plaques were moved below buildings and use player color, P-number and character portrait together, preventing the old plaque/building overlap and making ownership readable without relying on color alone.
- Hotel, mall and blessing-park upgrades now use three independently generated transparent building assets instead of reusing a generic level-five building.
- Purchasable land is now a dedicated low roadside parcel rather than a floating property card; characters retain their road coordinates while parcels and buildings occupy a separate roadside footprint.
- The current-player panel now exposes cash, bank deposit, net worth and a round-driven price index without opening a modal.
- The board action area now uses a dedicated AI-generated image-backed console, active-character portrait, grouped dice/card controls, animated turn banners, popup entrances and press/hover feedback.
- The home, character-selection stage, three map previews and three board worlds use dedicated scene art sized for the 16:9 virtual board camera.
- All ten CxQ characters have separate portrait art and two independently prepared walking poses used during real tile-to-tile movement.
- All six dice faces have dedicated throw art and appear as a physical multi-die overlay before movement.
- Every one of the twelve cards has its own cover. Each map has its own event scene.
- Roaming gods/NPCs, minigames, roadblocks, facilities, player seats and map fork signs use image assets rather than CSS-drawn substitutes.
- Each map has distinct Lv1-Lv5 property art and a landmark, with hotel, mall and park large-building identities and effects.

Game flow
- Purchase, upgrade, rent, net-worth and large-building calculations now share one round-driven market index and one canonical cost path for human players, AI players and UI previews.
- Passing the start tile pays the travel allowance exactly once; landing on start no longer duplicates that payment.
- Home -> character and P1-P4 seat setup -> map selection -> game conditions -> board -> result is one integrated state path.
- Seats support human, AI difficulty and empty states; active characters remain unique.
- Three maps use independent 36-node routes, facilities and economy multipliers. These authored oval-road maps intentionally expose no invisible shortcut branches.
- The board can be dragged and zoomed, with minimap, return-to-character camera and tile inspection.
- Turn phases include pre-roll cards and tools, one-to-three dice, animated movement, tile resolution and end-of-turn effects.
- Property play includes ownership markers, rent, shields, discounts, five upgrade levels, large-building choices and visible construction animation.
- Coupon shops separately buy and sell cards or tools; the card book holds fifteen cards and the tool box holds eight vehicles, roadblocks or bombs.
- Gods roam instead of occupying a fixed god tile; encounters visibly attach a companion to the character, replacement releases the former god, and expiration returns it to the road.
- Current-player data is restricted to the active human and includes overview, property camera jumps, card covers and timed effects.
- Saves migrate older card/state formats, and Safari/PWA assets share one cache revision.

Verification
- Automated checks cover all setup/game popup scenes, complete card/tool interactions, non-fixed god lifecycle, independent routes, multi-dice vehicles, save migration, five-level buildings, rent effects, three complete simulated matches, PWA asset parity and five landscape aspect ratios.
- Browser QA covers the published home, selection, map, conditions and live board flow with console/page-error inspection.
