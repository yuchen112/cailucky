CxQ Fairy-tale Richman — testing branch 20260825-0700

Release status
- This build belongs only to preview/cxq-fairytale-richman-testing.
- The main branch remains unchanged until the user approves the testing build.
- The only runtime path is index.html -> core.js + viewport.js + game.js. The obsolete home-richman4 overlay and temporary patch files are removed.

Art and presentation
- The home, character-selection stage, three map previews and three board worlds use dedicated scene art sized for the 16:9 virtual board camera.
- All ten CxQ characters have separate portrait art and two independently prepared walking poses used during real tile-to-tile movement.
- All six dice faces have dedicated throw art and appear as a physical multi-die overlay before movement.
- Every one of the twelve cards has its own cover. Each map has its own event scene.
- Roaming gods/NPCs, minigames, roadblocks, facilities, player seats and map fork signs use image assets rather than CSS-drawn substitutes.
- Each map has distinct Lv1-Lv5 property art and a landmark, with hotel, mall and park large-building identities and effects.

Game flow
- Home -> character and P1-P4 seat setup -> map selection -> game conditions -> board -> result is one integrated state path.
- Seats support human, AI difficulty and empty states; active characters remain unique.
- Three maps use independent 36-node routes, facilities, economy multipliers and branch choices.
- The board can be dragged and zoomed, with minimap, return-to-character camera and tile inspection.
- Turn phases include pre-roll cards, one-to-three dice, animated movement, branch selection, tile resolution and end-of-turn effects.
- Property play includes ownership markers, rent, shields, discounts, five upgrade levels, large-building choices and visible construction animation.
- Coupon shops buy and sell cards, banks move cash to deposits, and card capacity is fifteen.
- Gods roam instead of occupying a fixed god tile; attachment, replacement and timed positive/negative effects are active.
- Current-player data is restricted to the active human and includes overview, property camera jumps, card covers and timed effects.
- Saves migrate older card/state formats, and Safari/PWA assets share one cache revision.
